# 1. Project Vision

## Purpose

This project aims to build a premium, AI-powered English learning application designed exclusively for personal use.

This is not intended to become another language learning application.

Instead, it should become my personal English coach that learns about me over time, remembers everything, adapts to my progress, and helps me become fluent in English for both professional and everyday situations.

The application should be capable of accompanying me for many years, continuously improving my English without losing my history.

The application must behave as if it knows me personally.

Every interaction should make future lessons smarter.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Five years from now I expect this application to know:

- every mistake I usually make
- every word I have learned
- every grammar topic already mastered
- every pronunciation difficulty
- my favorite conversation topics
- my professional goals
- my study habits
- my strengths
- my weaknesses

Instead of asking me to study random lessons, it should know exactly what I need next.

---

# Mission

Create the most intelligent personal English tutor possible.

The application should combine:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Memory
- Voice Conversations
- Adaptive Learning
- Business English
- Long-term Progress Tracking

into one seamless experience.

---

# Success Definition

This project will be considered successful if, after using it consistently, I can:

• Participate confidently in meetings entirely in English.

• Conduct job interviews in English.

• Write professional emails without translation tools.

• Communicate naturally with coworkers from other countries.

• Give presentations in English.

• Travel internationally without communication problems.

• Continue improving for years without changing platforms.

# 3. Core Principles

Every decision made during development should respect these principles.

## Principle 1

The application must remember everything.

Nothing important should ever be forgotten.

---

## Principle 2

The application must become smarter every day.

Each conversation should improve future conversations.

---

## Principle 3

Learning must always be personalized.

No generic lessons.

No random exercises.

Everything should be generated specifically for me.

---

## Principle 4

Speaking is the highest priority.

The application exists primarily to improve spoken English.

Reading, writing and grammar should support speaking.

---

## Principle 5

Quality is more important than quantity.

Ten excellent exercises are better than one hundred mediocre ones.

---

## Principle 6

Everything should feel premium.

Animations

Transitions

Performance

Design

Voice

Interactions
